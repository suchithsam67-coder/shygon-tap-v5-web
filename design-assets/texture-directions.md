# AAROH texture directions: nine concepts, three selected (round 2)

> PROPOSED — REQUIRES VALIDATION. These are concept textures generated from geometry in `design-assets/src/build.mjs`. Each swatch is in `design-assets/textures/` (SVG) and `design-assets/renders/` (PNG); selected files end in `-selected`. Swatches show the whole pattern without the identity or calm zone, cropped from the upright card. Line widths and spacings are **artwork values**. The relief actually achieved (depth and width) depends on the process and is measured on prototypes.

All nine are original constructions: sine curves, stepped polygons, arcs, Bézier branches, and the iso-lines of a computed field. None is traced from an existing pattern, product or image.

Round 2 (2026-10-12) moved the cards to portrait and towards the moodboard's look: a lotus emblem, an architectural or botanical ornament in the lower half, and materials that read as real. Each edition's round-1 texture is kept as concept B for comparison.

## How they were compared

Each concept is scored from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong) on six criteria.

| Criterion | What it asks |
|---|---|
| Identity | Recognisably SHYGON: original, modern Indian, not generic |
| Edition fit | Matches the edition's colour and finish brief, and the moodboard |
| Tactility | Rewards touch and raking light, not only the eye |
| Calm centre | Leaves the emblem and name clear |
| Manufacturability | Line widths and spacing realistic for emboss, varnish or etch |
| Wear | Still reads after abrasion; no fine detail that fills or rubs off first |

Stepwell Diamonds remains a strong, more economical alternative (simpler die).
